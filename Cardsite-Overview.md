## What Cardsite is

Your audience arrives from QR codes, social profiles, search, messages, ads and shared links. Cardsite gives every one of those a home: a short URL that can be a page, a link, a file, a site or a card, hosted, routed, gated and measured in one place.

Every resource on Cardsite gets a slug. Every slug can live on card.site, on a subdomain, or on a domain you own.

## Card view

Add `@` before the slug of any public Cardsite URL to see that resource as a summary card, rendered for whoever is looking (device, language, referrer).

```
card.site/launch        → the resource
card.site/@launch       → its card
card.site/@alice        → the card of the bio page at card.site/alice
yourdomain.com/@brief   → the card of the brief
```

## URL lifecycle

Active resources stay at their address for as long as the account is active (Free) or the subscription is paid.

| | Free | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | 90 days with no sign-in and no visitor traffic | 90 days after the subscription lapses |
| Notices before release | 30 days, 7 days, 3 days, 24 hours | 90, 60, 30, 7, 3 days, 24 hours |
| After release | Address becomes available to others. Content served read-only at the archive address for 12 months. | Same. Archive address then reserved for the original owner until 3 years. |

The archive address follows one rule: drop the "a".

```
card.site/x        → crd.site/x
name.card.site/x   → name.crd.site/x
```

Reclaim: signing back in (Free) or resubscribing (Paid) during the archive window restores content and the archive address at once, and restores the original address if nobody has taken it.
